I design and build the website itself — structure, look and implementation — so it presents your business clearly and makes it easy for customers to reach you. Which pages it needs, the scope, the timeline and the price are agreed in consultation, before anything is built. This is a public-facing website, not internal management software: it shows your business to the world rather than running its day-to-day operations. Content production, hosting and ongoing maintenance are not part of the website itself; after launch, the separate [support service](/en/services#support-maintenance) is there if you need it.
