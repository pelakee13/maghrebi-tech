## Who I am

I'm Amin Maghrebi. I build tools that make sales and business-management processes simpler and automatic. From Telegram bots and management web apps to mobile apps, I take each project from idea to launch. I also design and build public-facing websites — business sites, landing pages and portfolios — that introduce a business and make it easy for customers to get in touch.

## What I work on

- Website design and development
- Process and sales automation
- Telegram bot design and development
- Smart business systems (web and mobile)
- Practical, project-based AI training
- Design, writing and programming

## How I work

- **Simple and practical:** I first understand the real problem, then build the smallest effective solution.
- **Transparent:** steps, timeline and cost are agreed in writing at the consultation.
- **Maintainable:** clean code and structure so the project can grow with your business.
- **Support after delivery:** maintenance is part of the work, not an add-on.

## Web3 research

Alongside client work, I research prediction markets, onchain structure and AI agents — how prediction markets are structured, how onchain data is organised, and where AI agents fit into that picture. This is research: reading, testing and writing about the space, not shipped client work.

## Background

Before moving fully into software, I spent years in financial and tax accounting. That experience taught me what businesses actually need, from reporting to inventory control, and helps me build systems that are genuinely useful.
