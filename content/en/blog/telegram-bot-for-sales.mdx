Most of your customers already have Telegram. That means nobody has to install a new app or create an account to get started.

## Three practical uses

1. **Answering repeated questions:** prices, opening hours and address are answered in seconds.
2. **Orders and appointments:** the customer enters details step by step and the order reaches you directly.
3. **Automatic reports:** daily or weekly sales figures are sent to you automatically.

## Where to start

Pick one manual process that repeats every day. Automate that first, measure the result, then move on to the next.
